# Customer interview — {{project}}

The Mom Test (Rob Fitzpatrick): ask about their life and past behaviour, not about our idea or their future intentions. 20–30 minutes, record only with permission.

## Before

- Who: one person from the best-fit segment; ideally someone who used the product this week, and someone who did not finish.
- Goal of this conversation, one line: what we want to learn.

## Questions (pick 5–7, follow the thread)

1. Tell me about the last time you [had the problem]. What happened?
2. Why was that hard? What did it cost you?
3. What did you do about it? What else did you try?
4. What do you like and dislike about those ways?
5. How did you find [product / the thing you use now]?
6. Walk me through your session with [product]: where did you slow down, what surprised you? *(users only)*
7. What almost made you stop? *(users who dropped)*
8. Who else do you know who deals with this? Could you introduce me?

Avoid: "Would you use…?", "Would you pay…?", "Do you like the idea…?", pitching. Compliments are not data; facts about the past are.
