# Research streams for the strategy step

Three `researcher` subagents in one message. Each gets: `.agents/product-marketing.md`, the founder's answers, markets and languages, and one stream below with its table. The agent's evidence rules apply: every row has a link, "not found" is a valid answer, ~10 minutes.

Search in each market's language (Russian queries for RU/KZ/CIS, English for global).

## A — where the audience lives

Question: where do the people from the ICP discuss the problem this product solves, and would a maker be welcome there?

Sources: Reddit (subreddit search, sidebar rules), Telegram (tgstat.ru / telemetr.io for channels and chats by topic), VK groups, Habr / VC.ru hubs, Discord servers, niche forums, Facebook groups, newsletters (Substack search), Product Hunt / Indie Hackers for maker-friendly spaces.

| Place | Market / language | Size and activity | What people ask there (quote + link) | Self-promo rules (quote + link) | Fit 🟢🟡🔴 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## B — creators and partners

Question: who already talks to this audience and could tell a story with the product?

Sources: YouTube search, Telegram channel catalogs (tgstat, telemetr), Instagram / TikTok hashtags (search results only), podcast directories, newsletter directories, complementary products (tools the ICP uses before or after this one).

| Creator / partner | Platform, market | Audience size, engagement signal | Why they fit (link to a relevant post) | Ads they ran before (link) | Contact route | Price signal | Tier (nano / micro / mid) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Prefer 10–20 nano and micro creators over a few large ones. Mark price signals as `гипотеза` unless published.

## C — how analogues got their first users

Question: what worked for competitors and similar products in their first months, by their own account?

Sources: founders' launch posts and retros (Indie Hackers, VC.ru, Habr, Medium, X search results), Product Hunt launch pages and comments, interviews and podcasts, their referral or affiliate programs, where they get backlinks and mentions.

| Product | Channel | What they did | Result they report (quote + link) | Transferable to us? |
|---|---|---|---|---|

Conclusion per stream: 2–3 lines; limitations line.
